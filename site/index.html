Why these particular rules
  • Similarity — near-identical bodies sent in a window is the fingerprint of bulk mail. This is the check that stops a mail merge with extra steps.
  • Tracking pixels — measurably lower inbox placement on cold mail, and they cut reply rates. Don't install Mailtrack, Streak, or Yesware.
  • Unsubscribe footers — required for marketing mail, which is exactly why one on a 1:1 email marks it as bulk. Use a human out instead.
  • Attachments — raise spam scores from unknown senders. Offer the CV; attach it once they reply.
  • Something specific — the one that matters. An email with nothing in it that is true only of the recipient is the one that gets reported.
